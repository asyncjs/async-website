On Thursday the 3rd of September Chris Villa will be giving us a glimpse into how he used AI within [Puck Editor](https://puckeditor.com) to allow you to build out pages using React and AI.

Every product is becoming AI-native, but creating an AI native product requires a new set of tools.

This talk will show you how to add an AI page builder to your existing product using Puck AI, letting your customers generate UI directly within your app.

It will primarily be a live-coding session, so expect lots of amusing typos, mishaps, and audience participation. We'll go deep into some of the APIs and have some fun along the way.

If time allows, we'll cover some lessons learnt building Puck's AI that you can apply to building your own agent.

Chris did his first talk introducing Puck at Async 3 years ago, which kicked off Puck's crazy journey to 13k stars and 1M monthly downloads. "I'm grateful for the opportunity to talk again."

You will be able to join us in-person at [Runway East](https://runwayea.st/locations/brighton?utm_source=external&utm_medium=event&utm_campaign=sponsorship) or online (link added closer to the event).
