On Thursday 3rd December we're very happy to be joined by Noam Rosenthal who works for Google in the Chrome team.

We'll be exploring the next generation of developments in HTML and the effect they'll have on how we build websites.

Unlike JS & CSS, HTML as a language remained relatively stable over the last decades. There are multiple proposals at various stages that aim to change that. Declarative partial updates, invokers, client-side include, declarative form validation, DOM localization... Let's see what those are, and imagine what HTML authoring would look like if they all materialized.
